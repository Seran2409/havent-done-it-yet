2. In short

  • The app works without an account. Your to-dos, routines, entries and settings are stored only on your device; in the web version, in your browser's storage.
  • We do not receive this content. There is no server of ours, no analytics or tracking tools and no advertising.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device, without a push server.

3. Data on your device

The app stores the titles and status of your to-dos and subtasks, your routines and entries, and your settings (optionally your first name, language, appearance, daily goal, reminder times). Its only purpose is to provide the app's features. This data does not leave your device unless you export it yourself.

You can delete everything in the app under Settings → “Delete all data”, by uninstalling the app or, for the web version, by clearing the site data in your browser.

4. Reminders

Reminders are only sent if you turn them on and allow notifications on your device. Their content is created on the device. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your system settings.

5. Export and import

Export creates a file with your data. Where you store it or whom you share it with is up to you; the terms of that service then apply.

6. Download via the App Store and Google Play

When you download the app, Apple or Google process data (such as your store account and download information) under their own responsibility. We have no influence on this. From the stores we receive aggregated statistics such as download numbers and ratings, and crash reports and usage statistics without direct personal reference if you agreed to share them with developers in your device settings. We use them to fix bugs and improve the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

7. Website and web version (GitHub Pages)

These pages and the web version are hosted by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit them, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, time of access, requested page and browser type to deliver and secure the pages (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). According to GitHub, it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, on which the transfer to the USA is based (Art. 45 GDPR). See GitHub's privacy statement.

The web version sets no cookies. It stores your content in your browser's local storage, which is technically required for it to work. Fonts ship with the app; no Google servers are contacted.

8. Contact by email

If you email us, we use your details only to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed and no legal retention period applies.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77). Since your app content stays on your device, you can view, export and delete it there yourself.

10. Changes

If new features are added, for example an account with sync, advertising or in-app purchases, we will update this policy before they launch.
